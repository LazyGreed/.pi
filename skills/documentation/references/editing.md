# Editing and anti-slop pass

Remove prefatory filler ('here is the thing', 'let us dive in'), empty contrast templates ('not X but Y' without genuine distinction), exaggerated praise, repetitive summaries, faux conversation, unnecessary headings, and opaque abstractions. Prefer concrete affected symbols, failure conditions, and user impact.

For reviews: precise bug/invariant, path/line, consequence, suggested fix. For architecture: proven friction, practical alternative, trade-off. For README: how to install/use, limitations, link to authoritative details. For commit messages: changed behavior, not model attribution.

Do not 'unslop' by shortening away important accuracy or breaking exact snippets, commands, quotes, schemas, legal text, or diagnostic strings. Maintain the intended register.
