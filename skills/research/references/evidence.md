# Evidence quality

Separate reproducible checks from anecdotal reports. Prefer versioned primary documentation and source; cite independent reports for ecosystem or user experience claims. For current claims, record retrieval date and product version. Avoid unstated extrapolation from demos or benchmarks.

For benchmarks, describe dataset, hardware, sample size, distribution, metric, warmup, and uncertainty before comparing numbers. For vendor claims, verify license, costs, dependencies, supported platforms, and current maintainer activity when relevant.

Stop when the decision has enough evidence, not when every adjacent topic is exhausted. Explicitly state which conclusion could change with new evidence.
