# Product-led visual taste

Begin with the product's signature object, workflow, or constraint. Pick a visual thesis and express it through a coherent palette, type scale, spacing rhythm, density, shape language, and one strong focal point. Distinctiveness requires a defensible relationship to the product, not decorative novelty.

Use adjustable art direction consciously: quiet vs expressive, compact vs spacious, static vs kinetic. Contrasts, alignment, proportion, hierarchy, and readable typography precede blur/gradients. Avoid ornamental badges, arbitrary metric cards, cloneable marketing templates, gratuitous 3D, and generic device mockups.

Review work at realistic content sizes, light/dark conditions, and worst-case states. Motion polish cannot compensate for missing functionality. Do not fabricate benchmarks, screenshots, user testimonials, or status badges.
